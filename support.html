Troubleshooting and FAQ

Photo Cleaner shows no photos, or only some of them

Photo Cleaner needs Full Access to your library. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Photo Cleaner and choose Full Access. With Limited Access, the app can only see the photos you picked.

Photos or videos load slowly

If iCloud Photos is on with Optimize iPhone Storage, some originals live in iCloud and iOS downloads them when needed. That needs a connection and can take a moment for big videos. Photos stored on the device load offline.

How does the Trash Bin work?

Swiping left moves a photo to the app's Trash Bin; nothing is deleted yet. Review it, restore anything you want to keep, then empty it. iOS shows its own confirmation dialog, and confirmed items move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for 30 days before removing them permanently.

I deleted something by mistake

If it is still in Photo Cleaner's Trash Bin, restore it there. If you already emptied the Trash Bin, open the Photos app → Recently Deleted (under Utilities), select the item and tap Recover. Items stay there for 30 days.

Why didn't my free space go up?

Deleted items sit in Recently Deleted for 30 days and still use space. To free it now, open Photos → Recently Deleted and delete them there. The iPhone Storage screen in Settings can also take a little while to update.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with, then use Restore Purchases in the app. Purchases are handled by Apple, so no account with us is needed.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Photo Cleaner. You can change plans or cancel there. Cancel at least 24 hours before a renewal or before the free trial ends to avoid being charged.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Cleaning, duplicate grouping and blur detection run on-device and work offline. A connection is only needed to buy or restore Pro, or to download originals stored in iCloud.

Photo Cleaner at a glance

Photo Cleaner is an iPhone and iPad app by Mohamed Elatabany for cleaning up your photo library: swipe left to delete and right to keep. It finds duplicates and similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots and large videos, and sends everything you delete to a Trash Bin you review first. It needs iOS 17 or later, is free to download (50 free deletions every week) with an optional Photo Cleaner Pro subscription, and processes everything on-device: no account, no photo uploads, no data collection.
