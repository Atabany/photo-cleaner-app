How to delete burst photos on iPhone and keep the best shot

Updated November 2026 · By Mohamed Elatabany

To delete burst photos on iPhone, open the burst in Photos, tap Select, tick the frames you want, tap Done, then choose Keep Only your favorites. The rest go to Recently Deleted for 30 days. To clean up many bursts at once, open the Bursts album and repeat per group. The built-in way is slow but fine for a handful; for hundreds, a swipe review is faster.

What is a burst photo, and why does it use so much space?

A burst is a rapid run of frames taken while you hold the shutter. On iPhones that support it, you can start one by holding Volume Up after turning on Settings → Camera → Use Volume Up for Burst (Apple's wording for iPhone XS, XR and later). Photos stacks the frames into a single thumbnail marked Burst, so a library can hold far more frames than the grid suggests. A burst of dozens of frames can be one thumbnail, and every frame is a full-size photo that counts toward storage.

How do I delete the extra frames from one burst?

  1. Open Photos and tap the burst thumbnail.
  2. Tap Select. The frames appear in a strip. Photos suggests some as likely best shots, but the choice is yours.
  3. Tap each frame you want to keep. You can keep more than one.
  4. Tap Done.
  5. Choose Keep Only your selected favorites (the label shows the number you picked). Choosing Keep Everything instead saves your picks as separate photos and also keeps the burst.

The frames you did not pick are moved to Utilities → Recently Deleted. They still use storage until that album is emptied. Apple's iPhone User Guide covers the Camera settings for bursts.

If the prompt never appears: some users on Apple Community report that Keep Only does not show up for a particular burst. Their workaround is to deselect your picks, tap Done, then select again and tap Done. That comes from user reports, not Apple.

How do I find all my bursts?

Photos collects them in a Bursts album under Media Types. On iOS 17, tap the Albums tab and scroll down. On iOS 18, scroll below the photo grid to the collections. On iOS 26, tap Collections. Menu positions differ a little between versions, so if you cannot see it, scroll the Albums or Collections screen to Media Types.

Photos has no button that keeps the best frame of every burst automatically. Each burst is a separate pass through the steps above.

How do I delete a whole burst I do not need?

  1. Open the Bursts album.
  2. Tap Select, tap the burst thumbnails you do not want, then tap the trash icon and confirm.

This deletes every frame in those bursts. If you want any frame from a burst, use the single-burst steps first.

Nothing is removed until you review the Trash Bin and confirm in the iOS dialog, and then iOS keeps the items in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Frequently asked questions

Does deleting burst photos free up storage?

Yes, but only after they leave Recently Deleted. Frames you drop stay there for 30 days. Open it, tap Select, then Delete All to free the space now. See how to empty or recover Recently Deleted.

Can I turn burst mode off?

Bursts only start when you hold Volume Up with the setting on. To stop the volume-button burst, switch off Settings → Camera → Use Volume Up for Burst.

Can I recover a burst frame I deleted?

Yes, within 30 days: open Utilities → Recently Deleted, select the photo and tap Recover. After that period, or once you empty the album, it is gone.

Why does my burst take so many photos?

The camera captures many frames per second while you hold the button, so even a short burst produces dozens of full-resolution photos.
