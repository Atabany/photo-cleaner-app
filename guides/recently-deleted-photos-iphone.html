How to empty or recover Recently Deleted photos on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By Mohamed Elatabany

Deleted photos and videos stay in the Recently Deleted album for 30 days, then iOS removes them for good. To free the space right away, open Photos → Utilities → Recently Deleted, unlock it, tap Select, then Delete All. To get a photo back, select it and tap Recover.

How do I empty Recently Deleted on iPhone?

  1. Open Photos, scroll down the Albums or sidebar view and tap Utilities → Recently Deleted.
  2. Unlock the album with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. On iOS 17 and later it is locked by default.
  3. Tap Select, then tap Delete All (or tick individual items and tap Delete).
  4. Confirm in the dialog. This is permanent: Photos has no undo after this step.

Each item also shows how many days it has left, so you can see what iOS will clear on its own. Apple documents this on its Recovering deleted photos page.

How do I recover a photo from Recently Deleted?

  1. Open Photos → Utilities → Recently Deleted and unlock it.
  2. Tap the photo or video and tap Recover, then confirm.
  3. For several items, tap Select, tick them, then choose Recover from the menu. The button labels can differ slightly between iOS versions, so follow what your screen shows.

Recovered items go back to your library, in their original place in your timeline.

Can I get photos back after emptying Recently Deleted?

Not from Photos. Once an item is deleted from Recently Deleted, or after its 30 days are up, the Photos app cannot restore it. Your remaining options are limited to copies that exist elsewhere:

  • An iPhone or iCloud backup made before the deletion. Restoring one returns the whole phone to that date, so anything added since is lost.
  • Copies you shared earlier: Messages, Mail, AirDrop, a computer or an external drive.
  • Another device on the same Apple Account only if it had not synced the deletion yet. With iCloud Photos on, a deletion syncs everywhere.

Apps that promise to undelete photos from the phone itself should be treated with caution. That is why reviewing before you empty matters.

Why is my iPhone storage still full after deleting photos?

  • They are still in Recently Deleted. Items there still use storage until removed. This is the most common cause.
  • The Storage screen lags. After clearing, restart the iPhone and reopen Settings → General → iPhone Storage; the figures can take a while to refresh.
  • Other things fill the phone. Apps, app data and System Data are counted separately from Photos. See iPhone storage full with iCloud Photos.
  • Optimize iPhone Storage is off. With iCloud Photos, that setting keeps full-size originals in iCloud and smaller versions on the phone.

Should I wait the 30 days or empty it now?

If you were careful about what you deleted, empty it and get the space back. If you deleted in a rush, scroll through the album first: it is your last safety net. Photo Cleaner builds that pause in. Swiping left only moves a photo to its own Trash Bin, nothing is deleted until you review the bin and confirm in the iOS dialog, and only then does the photo land in Recently Deleted for its 30 days.

What should I check before pressing Delete All?

Scroll the album once. It takes a minute and catches the usual regrets: a screenshot of a ticket or boarding pass, the only photo from an event, a video you meant to trim rather than delete. Tap Recover on those first. Anything you leave is gone for good the moment you confirm, and with iCloud Photos on, it is gone from your iPad and other devices too. FAQ

How long do photos stay in Recently Deleted?

30 days. After that iOS permanently deletes them automatically.

Why is Recently Deleted locked?

On iOS 17 and later, the album requires Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode, so someone holding your unlocked phone can't browse or purge it.

Does emptying Recently Deleted remove photos from iCloud?

With iCloud Photos on, deleting a photo removes it from your other devices on the same Apple Account, and emptying the album makes that permanent. iCloud Photos is sync, not a backup.

Can I skip Recently Deleted?

No. Photos always sends deletions there first; you can then delete them from the album immediately.
