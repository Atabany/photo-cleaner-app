How to mass delete photos on iPhone

Updated 4 November 2026 · By Mohamed Elatabany · Suggest a correction

To mass delete photos on iPhone, open your library or a collection in Photos, tap Select, drag your finger across the photos you want gone, then tap the trash button. They move to Recently Deleted for 30 days, so delete them from there too when you want the storage back.

How do I select many photos at once on iPhone?

The drag gesture does the work. Tapping each photo is what makes bulk deleting feel impossible.

  1. Open Photos and go to the grid you want to clear. On iOS 26 and 27, view your Library or open a collection. On iOS 18, swipe down to see the full photo grid. On iOS 17, use the All Photos or Days view, or open an album.
  2. Tap Select.
  3. Put your finger on the first photo and drag sideways along the row, then move down without lifting. Whole rows are selected as you pass over them, and the grid scrolls when you reach the edge of the screen.
  4. Tap any photo you want to keep to deselect it.
  5. Tap the trash button and confirm.

Apple documents the Select step for every supported iOS, and its iOS 17 guide spells out "tap or drag your finger on the screen". Pinch the grid to zoom out first: smaller thumbnails mean more photos under each drag, but look closely before you confirm.

Which grid should I delete from?

A smaller, filtered grid is safer than the whole library, because everything in it is already the kind of thing you meant to remove.

What should I check before a mass delete?

  • iCloud Photos. If it is on, the deletion applies to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. iCloud Photos is sync, not a separate backup. Read how device and iCloud storage differ.
  • A real copy. Before clearing a whole year, make sure anything irreplaceable also exists somewhere else, such as a computer or external drive.
  • Shared Library items. Deleting from an iCloud Shared Photo Library removes the item for every member, so check whose photos you are selecting.

How do I get the storage back?

A mass delete doesn't free space straight away, because the photos are held for 30 days. To clear them sooner:

  1. Open Collections → Utilities → Recently Deleted (on iOS 17, the Albums tab; on iOS 18, scroll down to Utilities).
  2. Tap View Album if you are asked to, and unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
  3. Tap Select, choose the items, then delete them.

This is the permanent step. The Recently Deleted guide covers recovering photos and what can't be undone.

When is selecting the wrong tool?

Drag-select is the fastest way to delete when you already know what should go: a whole trip of blurry parking-lot shots, or three years of screenshots. Photos is free, built in and enough for that. It is slower when each photo needs a decision, because a grid of thumbnails hides blur, bad framing and which of five similar shots is the good one. For that kind of backlog, one decision per full-size photo often works better; the camera roll guide has a ten-minute daily plan.

How to mass delete with Photo Cleaner

Photo Cleaner has no button that wipes your library. It builds up a batch from your decisions, then deletes that batch in one go.

  1. Open a slice from Home: Screenshots, Largest Files, Bursts, Live Photos, Similars / Duplicates, or a month under Months.

A worked example, including the free limit

The free allowance is 100 deletions, one time. Say you have used 40 of them and swiped 250 screenshots into the Trash Bin. You have 60 left, so Select All selects the first 60 and tells you so. The other 190 stay in the Trash Bin, not deleted, until you upgrade to Pro or recover them. If a bigger clear-out than that is all you need, the free drag-select in Photos has no such limit.

FAQ

Is there a Select All button in iPhone Photos?

Apple's iPhone guide doesn't document one for the Library, so drag to select there. If your screen shows Select All after you tap Select in a collection or album, you can use it. On a Mac, press Command-A in Photos to select everything in the current view.

How do I delete all photos from my iPhone but keep them in iCloud?

You can't while iCloud Photos is on, because a deletion syncs to iCloud and your other devices. To save space on the phone and keep the originals in iCloud, turn on Optimize iPhone Storage instead of deleting.

Why does dragging scroll instead of selecting?

Tap Select first, then start the drag sideways along a row. If your finger starts moving up or down straight away, the grid scrolls instead. Once a few photos are selected, move down and keep your finger on the screen.

Can I undo a mass delete?

Yes, for 30 days. Open Recently Deleted, tap Select, choose the items and recover them. After you delete them from Recently Deleted, or after the 30 days are up, Photos can't restore them.

Why is my storage still full after a mass delete?

Deleted items keep using space while they are in Recently Deleted. Delete them from there to get the space back, then give the iPhone Storage screen a little time to update.
