About Photo Cleaner and Its Developer

By Mohamed Elatabany · Updated 18 October 2026

I’m Mohamed Elatabany, the developer of Photo Cleaner: Swipe Cleanup. This is the app’s official website, with practical guides for reviewing an iPhone or iPad photo library.

What the app is for

Photo Cleaner helps you find photos and videos worth reviewing: duplicates and similar shots, blurry images, screenshots and large files. You decide what to keep. Marking an item for removal is followed by a review before confirmed deletion.

How these guides are written

I check app claims against its implementation and listing, and link Apple documentation for system behavior. Guides start with built-in options when they can solve the problem. Comparison pages are written by the maker of Photo Cleaner; they should be read with that relationship in mind.

Examples and calculator assumptions are illustrations, not measurements of your library. Neither the site nor the app can promise a particular amount of recovered storage.

Photos, privacy and deletion

Photo review happens on your device. Read the privacy policy for the complete statement. With iCloud Photos, confirmed library deletion can synchronize to other devices; read the iCloud storage guide before choosing a cleanup strategy.

Find a useful starting point

  • Estimate the file sizes you want to review
  • Try Apple’s duplicate-photo review first
  • Look for large videos

Contact and corrections

If a guide is unclear or a menu has changed, contact support. Please include the page and your iOS version; personal photographs are usually unnecessary for a correction.
