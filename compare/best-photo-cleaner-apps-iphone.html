Best photo cleaner apps for iPhone: an honest 2026 comparison

Updated October 2026 · By Mohamed Elatabany

There is no single best photo cleaner for everyone. Some people want to swipe through every photo; others just want duplicates gone. Here is an honest comparison of the free built-in option and five popular apps, including ours.

The apps, briefly

Apple Photos (built in)

Free and already on your iPhone. Since iOS 16, Utilities → Duplicates lists identical copies and lets you merge them, keeping the highest-quality version. It does not group similar shots, detect blur or sort by size, and deleting many photos means selecting them in a grid. Always worth doing first. How to use it.

Photo Cleaner

Ours. Swipe left to delete, right to keep, with a Trash Bin you review before anything is deleted. It also has a Similars / Duplicates finder that marks the best shot, Blur Detection, Largest Files, categories (Screenshots, Videos, Live Photos, Bursts, WhatsApp/Messenger), Memory Lane and a session recap. Everything runs on-device with no account. Free tier with a one-time allowance of 100 deletions, with an optional Pro subscription. iOS 17 or later.

Swipewipe

A swipe-based cleaner built around the same idea of quick keep-or-delete decisions. If swiping is all you need and you already like its interface, it is a reasonable choice. Swipewipe vs Photo Cleaner.

Slidebox

A long-standing swipe app. Its distinctive feature is sorting photos into albums while you review them, which neither Apple Photos nor Photo Cleaner does in the same way. A good fit if organising albums matters as much as deleting. Slidebox vs Photo Cleaner.

Cleanup

A storage cleaner focused on grouping duplicate and similar photos for bulk deletion. It suits people who prefer reviewing grouped results rather than swiping photo by photo.

CleanMy®Phone

From MacPaw, the makers of CleanMyMac. Its declutter tools group similar photos and duplicates and flag blurry shots and screenshots. A good fit if you already use MacPaw's tools.

How to choose

  • Only exact copies bother you: use the built-in Duplicates album. It's free.
  • You want to go through your whole library: a swipe app (Photo Cleaner, Swipewipe or Slidebox) is faster than the Photos grid.
  • You want to organise into albums as you go: Slidebox is built for that.
  • You want swiping plus duplicates, blur and large files in one app, entirely on-device: that's what Photo Cleaner is for.
  • You would rather pay once than subscribe: see photo cleaner apps with a one-time purchase.

Whatever you choose: check how deletion works before your first big session. With Photo Cleaner, swiped photos go to a Trash Bin first, then to iOS's Recently Deleted album for 30 days, so mistakes are recoverable.
