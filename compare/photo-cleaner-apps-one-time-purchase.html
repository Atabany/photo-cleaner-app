Photo cleaner apps for iPhone with a one-time purchase (no subscription)

Updated November 2026 · By Mohamed Elatabany

Yes, you can clean up your iPhone photos without a subscription. Of the well-known photo cleaners we checked on the US App Store on 8 November 2026, Photo Cleaner: Swipe Cleanup and Clever Cleaner sell a one-time lifetime purchase, Cleanup mentions a lifetime option in its description, and Remo Duplicate Photos Remover lists no in-app purchases at all. Photo Cleaner is the only app in the table whose listing documents swipe sorting, duplicates, blur detection and a lifetime purchase together, and it adds a Trash Bin you review before anything is deleted.

Why the delete flow matters as much as the price

Paying once usually means you keep the app for years and make thousands of quick decisions in it. Fast decisions are the point of a cleaner, so the safety net matters. In Photo Cleaner, swiping never deletes anything:

  1. Trash Bin. A left swipe moves the photo to the app's Trash Bin. You can look through it and restore anything with a tap.
  2. iOS confirmation. When you empty the Trash Bin, iOS shows its own dialog. Nothing is removed until you confirm.
  3. Recently Deleted. Confirmed photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for 30 days. How to recover or empty Recently Deleted.

Other apps describe their own safeguards: Cleanup shows a final review screen after each session, Clever Cleaner and CleanMy®Phone note that deleted items go to Recently Deleted, and Clever Cleaner has a Skip List for photos you never want included.

Is the built-in Photos app enough?

For exact copies, yes, and it costs nothing. Since iOS 16, Utilities → Duplicates in Photos finds identical copies and merges them, keeping the best-quality version. It does not group similar shots, detect blur or sort by size, so a large library still means a lot of manual selecting. Try it first: how to delete duplicate photos on iPhone.

Lifetime or subscription: how to decide

  • Use the free allowance first. Photo Cleaner gives you 100 deletions, one time, which is enough to see whether swiping suits you before you pay anything.
  • One big cleanup, then done: a single short subscription period may cost less than lifetime. Cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • A regular habit: a few minutes a week over years is where lifetime pays off, with no renewals to track. The 10-minute swipe method.
  • Check what lifetime includes. In Photo Cleaner, Lifetime unlocks the same Photo Cleaner Pro as the subscriptions: unlimited deletions and the premium tools.

How to buy Photo Cleaner's lifetime option

  1. Install Photo Cleaner and allow access to your photos.
  2. Tap PRO at the top of the Home screen, or open Settings and tap See Pro plans.
  3. Choose Lifetime ("Pay once, own forever") and confirm the purchase with Apple. The price is shown in your local currency before you confirm.
  4. On another iPhone or iPad, or after reinstalling, use Restore purchases in Settings with the same Apple Account.

FAQ

Is a lifetime purchase cheaper than a subscription in the long run?

Usually, if you keep using the app. Divide the lifetime price by the yearly price shown in the app: the result is how many years of subscription it equals. If you expect to use the app for longer than that, lifetime costs less. For a single cleanup, the free allowance or one short subscription period may be enough.

Can I switch from a Photo Cleaner Pro subscription to lifetime?

Yes. Buying lifetime does not cancel a subscription, so cancel it first in Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Photo Cleaner. Photo Cleaner shows its plans to people without active Pro, so when the paid period ends, open See Pro plans in the app's Settings and choose Lifetime.

What happens to Pro if I reinstall the app or get a new iPhone?

Purchases belong to your Apple Account, not to the app. Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Photo Cleaner's Settings and tap Restore purchases. No account with Photo Cleaner is needed. More in Support.

Does Photo Cleaner's free tier reset?

No. The 100 free deletions are a one-time allowance, not a weekly or monthly one, so they do not refill. Deleting and reinstalling the app on the same iPhone does not reset the count either.
