Your content

You decide what to delete. Photo Cleaner moves items to an in-app Trash Bin first, and deletion only happens after you empty the Trash Bin and confirm in the iOS dialog. Deleted items then stay in the Photos app's Recently Deleted album for 30 days. Please keep backups of photos that matter to you.

Photo Cleaner at a glance

Photo Cleaner is an iPhone and iPad app by Mohamed Elatabany for cleaning up your photo library: swipe left to delete and right to keep. It finds duplicates and similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots and large videos, and sends everything you delete to a Trash Bin you review first. It needs iOS 17 or later, is free to download (100 deletions free, one time) with an optional Photo Cleaner Pro subscription, and processes everything on-device: no account, no photo uploads, no data collection.
