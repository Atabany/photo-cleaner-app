Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

This policy covers the Photo Cleaner app for iPhone and iPad, made by Mohamed Elatabany. In short: your photos stay on your device, and the app collects no personal data.

Your photos

Photo Cleaner asks for access to your photo library so it can show your photos and videos, group duplicates and similar shots, detect blurry photos and sort by file size. All of this processing happens locally on your device. Photos and videos are never uploaded, copied to a server, or shared with anyone. Deletions use Apple's Photos framework, so iOS asks you to confirm and keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted.

Data we collect

None. Photo Cleaner has no account system, no analytics on your library and no advertising. Statistics such as streaks and storage freed are stored on your device only.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases of Photo Cleaner Pro are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details. The app uses RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription; RevenueCat receives the purchase information needed for that check, not your photos or personal content.

Ads and tracking

There are no ads and no tracking. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Email the developer via the App Support link on the App Store page.

Photo Cleaner at a glance

Photo Cleaner is an iPhone and iPad app by Mohamed Elatabany for cleaning up your photo library: swipe left to delete and right to keep. It finds duplicates and similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots and large videos, and sends everything you delete to a Trash Bin you review first. It needs iOS 17 or later, is free to download (50 free deletions every week) with an optional Photo Cleaner Pro subscription, and processes everything on-device: no account, no photo uploads, no data collection.
